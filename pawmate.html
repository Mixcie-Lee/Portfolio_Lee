WHAT IS PawMate?

PawMate is a mobile pet adoption platform developed by PawBridge to help connect animal shelters with people looking for their perfect pet companion. The application increases pet visibility and simplifies the adoption process through a digital platform that allows users to browse, discover, and connect with adoptable pets from shelters. Inspired by modern swipe-based applications, PawMate makes pet adoption more engaging and user-friendly while promoting responsible pet ownership.

How PawMate Works

PawMate makes pet adoption simple and engaging. From discovering adoptable pets to connecting with shelters, every step is designed to help users find their perfect companion with ease.

Discover Your Match

Browse adoptable pets through a swipe-based interface tailored to your preferences. Each swipe helps you discover pets that best match your lifestyle.

Learn About Each Pet

View detailed pet profiles, including age, breed, personality, vaccination status, and health information before deciding to adopt.

Connect & Adopt

Chat directly with participating shelters, ask questions, schedule visits, and begin the adoption process—all within the app.
